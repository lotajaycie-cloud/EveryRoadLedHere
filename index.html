Our Story

Every Road
Led Here

Ours has never been a story with a single turning point. It has been made of small, ordinary moments that quietly became everything. Long drives with the windows down. Coffee at the campsite before the sun was fully up. Bonfires that kept us talking long after we meant to sleep.

We found each other in the places in between, on the road, by the shore, somewhere between one adventure and the next. Somehow every path kept circling back to us.

Now, after years of choosing each other in the small moments, we're ready to choose each other for all of them.

On December 8, 2026, we'd love for you to be there as we say "I do."

Frequently Asked Questions

What should I wear?+

Formal, in light and muted shades. Ladies in floor length dresses in soft roses, dusty mauves and pale sages. Gentlemen in sages, sands and taupes, as a suit or a long sleeve shirt with trousers. Please avoid champagne, which is reserved for the bride.

Is the reception indoors or outdoors?+

The reception will be held at Lower Beach Club, styled in the open air with a view of the ocean. Comfortable shoes are recommended.
